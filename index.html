For privacy questions and requests, contact us at contactmelorekun@outlook.com.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to personal data processed by Hermes Agent, a Discord application that receives messages and requests in Discord and returns AI-generated text responses. It also explains the Operator’s processing of personal data in connection with the Service.

Discord processes data under its own privacy policy. This policy does not describe Discord’s processing (see section 8).

3. Personal data we process

Depending on how you use the Service, we may process the following categories of personal data:

  • Discord identifiers: your Discord user ID, username, and display name, and the identifiers of the server or channel where you interact with the bot.
  • Message content: the messages, prompts, and requests you send to the bot, including any information you choose to include in them.
  • AI responses: the text responses generated by the bot in reply to your messages.
  • Agent data: conversation history, memory, and reusable procedures (“skills”) that Hermes Agent stores when conversations are kept, which may include personal data contained in your messages.
  • Technical logs: timestamps, error messages, and technical event data needed to operate, troubleshoot, and secure the Service.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal data. Please do not send passwords, payment card details, government identification numbers, or health or other sensitive information unless it is strictly necessary.

4. Where the data comes from

Most personal data is provided directly by you when you send messages to the bot in Discord. Discord also provides us with your user identifiers and display information when you interact with the bot. Technical logs and agent data are generated by the Service during its operation.

5. Purposes and legal bases

We process personal data for the following purposes:

  • To receive your requests and provide responses in Discord. Legal basis: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, where processing is necessary to respond to a request you make.
  • To maintain conversation context and memory, where configured. Legal basis: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, to the extent this is needed to provide the Service you requested, and Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in providing a coherent service, balanced against your rights.
  • To operate, troubleshoot, and secure the Service, including through technical logs. Legal basis: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in keeping the Service working and secure.
  • To respond to privacy requests and comply with legal obligations. Legal basis: Article 6(1)(c) GDPR, where a legal obligation applies.

This is a general description of the legal bases the Operator currently considers applicable. It is not a legal certification.

6. How AI processing works

Current configuration: the AI model runs locally through Ollama. In the current configuration, your prompts and the bot’s responses are not sent to external AI providers.

Hermes Agent is open-source software. According to its documentation, it does not collect telemetry, usage data, or analytics, and its model requests go only to the endpoint configured by the operator, which in this case is the local Ollama instance. Conversation history, memory, and skills are stored locally on the infrastructure operated by the Operator.

If the Operator later connects the Service to an external AI provider, this policy will be updated before the change takes effect, and the provider will be listed as a recipient.

AI responses are generated automatically and are not used to make decisions that produce legal effects concerning you or that similarly affect you significantly.

7. Data retention

Messages, prompts, AI responses, technical logs, and agent data (including conversation history, memory, and skills) are kept until they are deleted by the Operator, or until we delete them following a verified request under section 14.

No fixed retention period has been defined yet. [RETENTION PERIOD TO BE DEFINED BY THE OPERATOR, e.g. a number of days for logs and conversation history] Data that is no longer needed for the purposes described above will be deleted.

8. Recipients and third-party services

Personal data is processed by the following services:

  • Discord (Discord Inc., United States): the platform through which you interact with the bot. Discord processes messages and account data under its own privacy policy and terms.
  • Ollama: runs the AI model locally on infrastructure operated by the Operator. It does not send your data to a third-party AI provider in the current configuration.
  • Hermes Agent: open-source software that runs on infrastructure operated by the Operator. Its documentation states that it does not collect telemetry, usage data, or analytics.

Apart from the services listed above, we do not currently share your personal data with other recipients. We do not sell personal data.

9. International transfers

The processing performed by the Service itself takes place on infrastructure operated by the Operator, under the current configuration described above. Discord may transfer and process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in Discord’s privacy policy.

If the Operator uses a service located outside the European Economic Area in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly.

10. Security

The Service runs on infrastructure operated by the Operator, and access to the bot is limited to users the Operator authorizes. No system is completely secure, so please avoid sending information that you would not want to be stored or misused.

We do not knowingly process personal data of users below those ages. If you believe that a user below the applicable age has provided personal data, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

12. Cookies, analytics, and tracking

13. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable law, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased, where the conditions of the law are met;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • receive your data in a portable format, where applicable;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal;
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Italy, this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

14. How to exercise your rights

To make a request, email contactmelorekun@outlook.com with the subject “Privacy request” and include your Discord user ID or username and a description of your request.

We may ask for additional information to confirm your identity, but only where it is necessary and proportionate. We will respond without undue delay and, in any case, within one month of receiving your request, as required by Article 12(3) GDPR. This period may be extended where permitted by law, and we will inform you if it is extended.

Where we delete personal data stored by the Service, we will do so unless retention is required by law.

15. Limits on data held by Discord

Discord stores messages and account data on its own servers under its own privacy policy. We cannot delete data that Discord holds independently of the Service. For requests about Discord’s data, please contact Discord directly. You can also delete your own messages in Discord at any time.
